Account Deletion — In-Play FOOTBALL MANAGER

App: In-Play FOOTBALL MANAGER, package com.ilikeit.ipfm
Developer: ZUNOZ · Contact: juno.lab77@gmail.com

1. Delete in the app

Go to Settings → Account → Unlink Google. The following data is deleted immediately.

DeletedDetails
Multiplayer profileManager name, portrait number, club, rating
PlaybookYour uploaded squad and tactics
Challenge recordsMatch records stored under your account
Sign-in accountThe Firebase account created through Play Games
Cloud backupThe save backup stored in Play Games

What remains: daily play-count statistics (not identifiable), and results with your manager name shown in other users' match records. Request deletion of these by email (section 2). Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Crashlytics) are anonymous and not linked to your account.

2. Request by email without the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email juno.lab77@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • Your Play Games nickname (the manager name used in the game)

We delete all data in the table above and your manager name from other users' records within 30 days of the request and reply when done. Deletion cannot be undone.
