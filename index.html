Privacy Policy

In-Play FOOTBALL MANAGER · Effective 21 August 2026

In short. This game runs no server of its own, and the developer stores none of your personal information. Game progress is saved on your device and, only if you choose, backed up to your own Google account through Play Games Saved Games. The game does not access your name, email address, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or device storage.

1. What is collected

By the developer: nothing. There is no account registration and no developer-operated server.

On your device only: career progress, squad and tactics, match records and settings. If you sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games display name is shown in the interface for the duration of the session; it is not stored.

By third-party services included in the game:

Firebase is used anonymously only: this game never passes it a user identifier (no setUserId), so what it shows the developer is aggregate numbers, not people.

2. Retention

Device data is removed when you uninstall the game. Cloud backups live in your own Google account and are removed when you delete this game's saved data in the Play Games app. Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies.

3. Sharing and international transfer

The developer does not sell or share your information. The services listed above collect data directly and process it on servers outside the Republic of Korea, including the United States. You can limit this by resetting or opting out of your advertising ID and by disconnecting your Google account in the game's settings.

4. Permissions

The game requests INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION. No runtime-sensitive permissions (camera, location, storage, contacts) are requested.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information without parental consent, please contact us at the address below.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or opt out of your advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Disconnect your account: in-game Settings → Account.
  • Delete cloud saves: Play Games app → Profile → Settings → Delete game data.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the game, or clear its app data.
